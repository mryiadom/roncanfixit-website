TV Mounting FAQs

Can you mount a TV on any wall type?

Yes — brick, plasterboard, concrete and stud walls are all fine. The right fixings are used depending on the wall, confirmed before work starts.

Do you supply the bracket and materials?

Materials including the bracket can be provided, or your own bracket can be fitted if you already have one.

What size TVs can be mounted?

Any size, from small bedroom screens to large home cinema displays, with the correct fixings used for the size and wall.

How long does it take?

Most jobs take 45-90 minutes depending on TV size and wall type.
